🎯
predict_mbbs_seat
NEET AIR / State Rank ➔ College & Margin

🏥
get_college_info
College Stats & Distances

Fetches intake capacity, tuition fees, hospital distance from Hyderabad, and historical closing ranks for any Government or Private medical college.

compare_colleges
Side-by-Side College Analysis

Performs comprehensive comparative analysis between two colleges (fee difference, travel distance, cutoffs, and hospital clinical exposure).

📈
calculate_sliding_odds
Round 1 ➔ Round 2 Upgradation

Computes mathematical probability of upgrading from a Round 1 confirmed seat to a dream college based on category rank and surrendered seats.
