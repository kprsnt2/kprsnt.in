ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

How I Build Reliable Autonomous Systems

Most agentic systems fail in production due to compounding hallucinations, unchecked token burn, or state drift. Here is how I engineer for enterprise reliability:

1. Deterministic Cost Gates

Never send unfiltered noise to expensive LLMs. JobAgents scores all 9,697 sourced jobs deterministically with zero model calls, so only the top-N candidates ever reach a model.

2. Forensic Hash Ledgers

Agents cannot self-attest success. Immutable hash-chained audit ledgers kept outside agent write trees prevent stealth overwrites and ensure cryptographic reproducibility.

3. Regulated Compliance Rigor

9 years of federal CMS compliance auditing at Optum applied to AI systems: scope boundaries, per-turn tool-call audit, and promotion gates that refuse unverified output.
